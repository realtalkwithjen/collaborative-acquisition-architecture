# System: Systematic Deal Screening

## Definition
The operational process for qualifying real estate deals before execution.

## Components
- Pre-screening call triage
- Deal Analyzer Template
- "No Offer" Protocol
- Conservative math validation (50–65% rule)

## Function
Filters motivated seller opportunities through a disciplined, risk-aware lens.

## Application Context
Used at the top of the student journey and before any negotiation begins.

## What It Replaces
Hope-based math and volume-driven acquisition pressure.
