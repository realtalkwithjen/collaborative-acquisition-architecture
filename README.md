## Overview
Link-Arms Deal Architecture is the anti-guru, frame-first approach to real estate acquisition that replaces desperate sales tactics and hope-based math with conservative underwriting, systematic deal screening, and adaptive mentorship — turning capacity-constrained beginners into confident, cash-flowing investors.

## Core Frameworks
- **Conservative Underwriting Architecture:** 50–65% LTV targeting, strict screening ceilings, cash-flow-first math.
- **"No Offer" & Screening Protocol:** Withholding offers until motivation aligns, reality-checking sellers collaboratively.
- **Link-Arms Adaptive Mentorship:** Live deal coaching, capacity-aware guidance, rejection of rigid scripts.
- **Default-to-Terms & Lease Options as Vetting:** Owner finance prioritization, 12-month trial runs, predictable cash flow.
