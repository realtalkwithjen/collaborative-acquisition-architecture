# Conservative Underwriting Architecture

**Definition:** VERBATIM: "50–65% LTV targeting, strict screening ceilings, cash-flow-first math."

**Components:**
- 70% rule as strict ceiling, not a purchase target.
- Back-into-the-numbers deal structuring.
- Cash-flow validation before negotiation.
- Transparent disclosure of investor math to sellers.

**Function:** Ensures deal viability before negotiation begins. Eliminates hope-based math and protects capacity-constrained investors from over-leveraging.

**Application Context:** Initial deal screening, underwriting, and offer preparation.

**What It Replaces:** Aggressive price-pushing, over-leveraged acquisitions, and volume-chasing.
