# Default-to-Terms & Lease Options as Vetting

## Definition
Prioritizing owner finance, sub-to, and "some now, some later" exits. 12-month lease options serve as trial runs to verify payment history before closing.

## Components
1. Owner finance structuring as primary exit
2. Sub-to title company navigation protocols
3. 12-month lease option vetting period
4. "Some now, some later" payment flexibility

## Function
Reduces upfront capital requirements, verifies seller/buyer reliability, and creates predictable cash flow streams.

## Application Context
Applied when seller motivation is high but cash flow is the priority over quick equity.

## What It Replaces
All-cash pressure tactics, speculative flipping, and high-down traditional financing models.
