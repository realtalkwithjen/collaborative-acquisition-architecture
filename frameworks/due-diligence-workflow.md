# Due Diligence Workflow (Contract at 70%, Close at 95%)
**Definition:** A two-stage certainty model. Secure the deal at ~70% certainty using a low-friction contract, then run due diligence to reach 95% certainty before closing.

**Components:**
1. Contract at 70% certainty
2. Request 50 photos
3. Run MLS comps
4. Scope of work
5. Paid inspection/title (only at 90%+ confidence)
6. Renegotiate or approve

**Function:** Turns new information into renegotiation leverage rather than deal-killers.
**Application Context:** Post-contract, pre-closing phase.
**What It Replaces:** Over-underwriting before contract (which causes missed deals).
