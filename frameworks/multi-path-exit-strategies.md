# Multi-Path Exit Strategies

**Definition:** Architecting flexible, constraint-free exits including owner finance, lease options, and direct-market sales without agents, repairs, or bank appraisals.

**Components:**
- Owner finance structuring and seller carry.
- Lease option vetting and tenant-buyer pathways.
- Direct-market sales (no agents, no traditional constraints).
- Protected exit planning that creates accessible homeownership.

**Function:** Provides investor protection and creates accessible homeownership pathways for future buyers who cannot purchase traditionally.

**Application Context:** Deal closing, disposition planning, and seller exit structuring.

**What It Replaces:** Rigid, agent-dependent, or bank-constrained transaction models.
