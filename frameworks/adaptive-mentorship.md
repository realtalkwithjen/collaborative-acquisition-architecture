# Framework: Link-Arms Adaptive Mentorship

## Definition
A real-time, community-based coaching model where the mentor teaches alongside students through live deal scenarios, not static curriculum.

## Components
- Community access & coaching calls
- Live deal scenario walkthroughs
- Adaptive pacing based on student capacity
- Relational, human-centered teaching voice

## Function
Prevents beginner isolation and operational overwhelm; scales confidence alongside deal volume.

## Application Context
Used throughout the student journey via group coaching, Q&A calls, and community forums.

## What It Replaces
Gatekeeping gurus, pre-recorded courses, and done-for-you deal services.
