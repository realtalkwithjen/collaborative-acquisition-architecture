# The "No Offer" & Screening Protocol

**Definition:** VERBATIM: "Withholding offers until motivation aligns, reality-checking sellers collaboratively."

**Components:**
- Frame-first opening with third-party facts (comps, market data).
- Motivation alignment check before price discussion.
- Transparent walk-away if frameworks do not align.
- Honest redirection to alternative paths if the deal is not viable.

**Function:** Establishes deal reality before price discussion. Filters mismatched sellers and builds earned trust through transparency.

**Application Context:** Seller negotiation and initial screening calls.

**What It Replaces:** Script-reliance, high-pressure sales tactics, and premature offers.
